    Interface¶

    The Textevo panel adapts to its size: dock it vertically or horizontally.

    From left to right (or from top to bottom): Add, Remove, Rename, Based on, 2D / 3D, Fx, Copy, Paste, Presets and Settings. Hover a button to see what it does.

    Most buttons work on the selected Textevo effects, or on all the Textevo of the selected text layers when no Textevo is selected.

    Always use the Remove and Rename buttons

    A Textevo is made of two parts: the Textevo effect and a text animator on the text layer, linked by expressions. Removing or renaming the effect by hand only changes one of the two parts. Use the Remove and Rename buttons of the panel, which handle both.

    Add¶

    Adds a Textevo to the selected text layer. If no text layer is selected, Textevo creates one in the active composition.

    • Click: keyframes on the Strength from 100 % to 0 %, starting at the current time (an arrival).
    • Alt + click: the reversed animation, from 0 % to 100 %, with mirrored easing (an exit).

    The keyframes, their easing, the delay and the direction of a new Textevo are set in the settings.

    Remove¶

    Removes the selected Textevo, or all the Textevo of the selected layers: the effect and its text animator.

    If you delete the effect by hand, its text animator stays on the layer with expression errors, and has to be deleted by hand too.

    Rename¶

    Renames the selected Textevo effect and its text animator together.

    If you rename the effect by hand, After Effects updates the expressions but not the name of the text animator: the effect and its animator no longer have the same name in the timeline.

    Based on¶

    Chooses how the text is broken down: Letters, Letters spaceless, Words or Lines.

    Auto adapt the delay: adjusts the delay to the new number of letters, words or lines, to keep about the same total duration. For example, switching from 12 letters to 3 words multiplies the delay by 4.

    2D / 3D¶

    Switches the 3D per character of the layer on or off. The Textevo animators are rebuilt with the 2D or 3D properties (3D rotations, Z position...), keeping your settings and what you added to them.

    Fx¶

    Adds an Elastic, a Bounce or an Oscillation to the strength of the Textevo. See Fx.

    Copy¶

    Copies the selected Textevo, or all the Textevo of the layer: the effects with their keyframes, their Fx, their "Based on" mode and what you added to their text animators.

    Paste¶

    Pastes the copied Textevo on all the selected text layers.

    • Click: the keyframes keep their position from the start (in point) of the layer: a keyframe on the first frame of the copied layer lands on the first frame of the target layer.
    • Alt + click: the first keyframe lands at the current time.

    Presets¶

    • Click: opens the presets window.
    • Shift + click: saves the Textevo of the selected layer as a preset.

    Settings¶

    Opens the settings: the default values of a new Textevo, the presets folder...
