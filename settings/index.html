    Settings¶

    Click the Settings button of the panel.

    Change the values, then click Save. Cancel closes the window without saving, Reset puts back the default values (saved when you click Save).

    Add button¶

    The values used when you click Add.

    Add keyframes on Strength
    Adds two keyframes on the strength of the new Textevo: from 100 % to 0 %, or from 0 % to 100 % with Alt + click on Add. Unchecked, the Textevo is added without keyframes.
    Duration (seconds)
    The time between the two keyframes.
    Keyframe influence (%)
    The easing of the two keyframes: the In and Out influence of the First key and of the Second key, from 0 (linear) to 100. With Alt + click on Add, the easing is mirrored.
    Delay (frames between characters)
    The delay of the new Textevo.
    Direction
    The direction of the new Textevo.
    Based on : auto adapt the delay
    When you change the "Based on" mode, adjusts the delay to the new number of letters, words or lines.

    Fx¶

    Elastic / bounce on eased keys
    What to do with the keyframes eased on arrival when an Elastic or a Bounce is set: Ask, Keep the keys, Linear arrival or Optimized keys. See Eased keyframes.

    Presets folder¶

    Where Textevo saves its settings and presets. Open shows the folder, Change... chooses another one.

    Note

    Changing the folder doesn't move your presets: copy the presets folder to the new place if you want to keep them.
